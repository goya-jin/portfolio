Gentle Monster Pocket collection x Bratz
Home ↑

Role   Digital Contents Creative, Art director
Year   2025Planned and produced digital content for the Gentle Monster × Bratz collaboration, focusing on viral engagement. Contributed to the creative direction for campaign imagery by Charlotte Wales and the film by Suho Lee (Boring Studio). Directed conceptual product visuals, pop-up space content, and celebrity assets, visualizing a 'Romantic Humanoid' motif by merging the product's foldable mechanics with robotic aesthetics. The collaboration with Karina (aespa) resulted in a viral Instagram video, achieving 17.3M views and 625K likes.
