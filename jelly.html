Role   Digital Contents Creative, Art director
Year    2024Participated in the digital creative direction of the Jelly Collection project, exploring surreal beauty and an otherworldly allure inspired by the “Dark Romantic Third Kind Alien” concept. Collaborated with Elizaveta Porodina on the campaign imagery, and developed 3D teaser concepts, conceptual product visuals, and pop-up space content. By focusing on sensory atmosphere rather than product exposure, the project helped shape the brand’s experimental image.
