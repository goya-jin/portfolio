Maison Margiela x Gentle Monster 3rd chapter
Home ↑

Role   Digital Contents Creative, Art director, 
            Video Production
Year    2025
Planned digital content for the 3rd chapter of the Maison Margiela X Gentle Monster collaboration and directed digital creative assets, including campaign visuals captured by Jordan Hemingway. Collaborated with the in-house 3D team on teaser content and developed conceptual product imagery to align with the overarching narrative. Conceptualized the collection by depicting the contrasting metallic, bold frames and classical pieces as an encounter between figures from two distinct worlds.
