16 freeze frames

I took this photo with a Fujifilm Rensha Cardia BYU-N 16, a 16-lens 35mm film camera originally designed to help golfers improve their swing. I divided the photograph into 16 frames and stitched them together. I first turned the sequence into a short GIF animation, then transformed it back into a still image. I think the way these layers overlap resembles how time and memory work.
